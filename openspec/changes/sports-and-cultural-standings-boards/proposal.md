## Why

The overall board must communicate both each sport's competitive table and the wider section race. Sport-specific statistics and explicit tie-breakers make rankings understandable, while distinct sports and cultural totals preserve the different scoring systems.

## What Changes

- Add a standings table for each sport using its relevant performance statistics and tie-break rules.
- Add one combined cultural points board and one combined sports points board, showing per-event points by section.
- Separate sports and cultural totals on the main scoreboard.
- Recalculate rankings only from approved results; order tied totals by event-specific tie-break criteria.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `competition-standings`: Sport-specific, cultural, sports-total, and overall standings.

### Modified Capabilities
- `live-scoring`: Approved result outcomes update all applicable standings and tie-break statistics.

## Impact

Standings views and calculation utilities, fixture scorecards/result approval, and event points policy configuration. No new external dependencies are expected.
